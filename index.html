Token Architecture

How A7A5 Works

A7A5 is a ruble-pegged stablecoin engineered to move Russian ruble value across international blockchain rails while keeping its reserve base entirely inside Russia — beyond Western freezing authority.

Reserve Backing
Each A7A5 token is backed 1:1 by Russian rubles held in custodial accounts at Promsvyazbank (PSB) — a state-owned, OFAC-sanctioned Russian defense bank. Reserves are structurally unreachable by Western courts or asset freeze orders.
OFAC-SANCTIONED CUSTODIAN
Minting & Burning
A7 LLC (operating as Tokeon) mints new A7A5 tokens when rubles are deposited into PSB. Redemption burns tokens and returns rubles. This mint/burn cycle is the primary mechanism for converting sanctioned ruble proceeds into blockchain-transferable value.
FINANCIAL GATEWAY
Cross-Chain Transfer
A7A5 is issued on both Tron (TRC-20) and Ethereum (ERC-20). Tron is the primary volume chain due to near-zero fees. Cross-chain hops via bridges break simple wallet-to-wallet tracing and add layering complexity.
DUAL-CHAIN ISSUANCE
50% Yield Incentive
A7A5 offered a 50% annual yield on staking — an economically irrational rate for a legitimate stablecoin. This was a deliberate liquidity incentive to attract crypto-native market makers and OTC desks into the ecosystem, bootstrapping the $2B+ liquidity pool seeded by A7 LLC.
MARKET MANIPULATION
The Three-Layer A7A5 Architecture
LAYER 1 — RESERVE
Promsvyazbank Moscow holds all ruble reserves. Token value is guaranteed by the Russian state bank's balance sheet. Completely immune to Western enforcement. No Tether-style freeze mechanism possible.
LAYER 2 — ISSUANCE
Old Vector LLC / A7 LLC (Tokeon) in Kyrgyzstan is the formal legal issuer. Kyrgyzstan's FATF grey-list status and weaker AML framework make it the ideal off-shore jurisdiction for regulatory arbitrage.
LAYER 3 — CIRCULATION
Garantex / Grinex exchanges and OTC desks convert A7A5 ↔ USDT ↔ fiat. Grinex was pre-positioned before Garantex sanctions, ensuring circulation continuity. The Hong Kong node services Chinese payment corridors.

Reserve Infrastructure

Promsvyazbank —
The Reserve Fortress

PSB is the single most critical node in the A7A5 architecture. As a fully state-owned Russian defense bank, it transforms A7A5 from a commercial crypto project into a sovereign financial weapon.

PSB Institutional Profile
⚠
Full State Ownership
PSB was nationalized in 2018 under direct Kremlin order after near-collapse. It is now wholly owned by the Russian state and operates primarily as the bank for Russian defense contractors and military-industrial complex payments.
⚠
OFAC SDN + EU Sanctions
PSB has been under comprehensive OFAC SDN designation since February 2022 (Ukraine invasion sanctions). All transactions with PSB by US persons are prohibited. EU similarly blacklisted PSB, cutting off SWIFT access.
⚠
A7A5 Reserve Custody
PSB holds all ruble reserves backing A7A5 tokens in circulation. This is by design — reserves inside PSB are structurally immune to Western asset freeze orders. No court order, no Tether-style instruction, no SWIFT block can reach PSB's balance sheet.
⚠
Defense Payments Dual-Use
PSB simultaneously processes conventional ruble-denominated defense contractor payments and A7A5 crypto minting operations — creating a deliberate regulatory ambiguity between a domestic banking function and international sanctions evasion.
Why PSB Was Chosen
The architects deliberately selected PSB over commercial Russian banks. PSB's state ownership means it cannot be pressured by foreign correspondent banks, cannot be cut off from Russian state liquidity, and has implicit FSB/GRU protection. It is the most sanction-proof institution available.
The Jurisdictional Gap
Traditional stablecoin issuers (Tether, Circle) hold reserves in US banks, money market funds, or T-bills — all reachable by DOJ and OFAC. PSB reserves in rubles inside Russia exist in a completely separate legal universe. OFAC can designate A7A5 itself, but cannot freeze the backing reserves.
